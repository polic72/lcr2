# Lethal Company Randomizer
A recreation of the [lcr mod by nicofloflo](https://github.com/nicofloflo/lcr).


## What it randomizes:
LCR2 randomizes the following features:
- Level Scrap
    - Each moon you land on may have the scrap of any other moon.
    - Gordion scrap will never happen.
- Indoor Enemies
    - Each moon you land on may have the indoor enemies of any other moon.
- Outdoor Enemies
    - Each moon you land on may have the outdoor enemies of any other moon.
    - Liquidation outdoor enemies will never happen.
